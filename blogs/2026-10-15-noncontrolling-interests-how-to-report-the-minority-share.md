Noncontrolling interest accounting under ASC 810-10-45 reports the minority share inside consolidated equity, separate from the parent's equity, never as a liability. Consolidated revenue and expenses include 100 percent of the subsidiary, and the split happens only below consolidated net income. Indinero's CPA-led team documents the attribution basis for each partially owned subsidiary, with bookkeeping, GAAP close, and business tax in one engagement.
