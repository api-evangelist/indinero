A financial KPI dashboard is the tight set of financial and operational measures a leadership team steers by, typically seven to 12 metrics that pair leading and lagging indicators. It answers operating questions a profit and loss statement can't, like where margin is drifting and how fast cash converts. Indinero's CFO services design that dashboard on the books our accounting team already maintains, with continuous operations since 2009.
