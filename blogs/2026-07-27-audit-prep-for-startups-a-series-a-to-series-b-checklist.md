Audit prep for startups is the work of getting your books, supporting schedules, and documentation ready so an independent CPA firm can sign an opinion on your GAAP financials. Most venture-backed companies hit their first audit between Series B and pre-IPO. Indinero has prepared dozens of startups for first audits with clean PBC packages, handled inside the bundled monthly engagement rather than as a separate carve-out.
