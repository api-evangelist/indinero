Fractional CFO readiness is a property of your reporting, not a verdict on your business. It reduces to five conditions: a close on a predictable schedule, revenue and cost in the right period, a chart of accounts that separates the segments leadership manages, honest receivables and payables, and consistent treatment month to month. Indinero keeps the CFO and accounting work coordinated, so the reporting is designed toward a known target while cleanup happens.
