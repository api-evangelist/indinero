The first 90 days with a fractional CFO move through three phases. Days 1 to 30 diagnose how the business makes money and where cash gets trapped, days 31 to 60 build the leadership reporting rhythm and first rolling forecast, and days 61 to 90 point that work at one real decision. Indinero sequences that arc by operational complexity, coordinating CFO work with the team closing your books.
