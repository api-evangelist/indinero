ARR vs GAAP revenue will never tie, because ARR is a non-GAAP operating metric measured at a point in time and GAAP revenue is recognized across the period under ASC 606. A bridge with named lines for mid-period starts, churn timing, contracted-not-live deals, services, and usage explains the gap. Indinero brings 100+ years combined team experience to that bridge, building it inside every monthly close rather than under deal pressure.
