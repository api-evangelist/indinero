A rolling forecast projects a fixed number of months ahead, re-extending each period as one month closes and a new one gets added, so the forward view never shrinks. Only 43% of organizations use one, according to AFP's 2026 FP&A benchmarking survey. Indinero's CFO team builds and maintains rolling forecasts on the same closed books its accounting team keeps.
