CFO advisory services give a business ongoing, forward-looking financial leadership without a full-time executive hire, covering profitability by segment, rolling cash forecasting, decision modeling, and multi-year value building. At indinero it runs as a distinct engagement coordinated with the accounting and tax teams, so the CFO advisor uses the numbers but doesn't produce the books or file returns. Fit is set by operational complexity, not revenue or funding stage.
