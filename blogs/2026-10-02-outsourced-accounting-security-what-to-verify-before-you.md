Outsourced accounting security comes down to three verifiable things: the SOC 2 report and its scope, which access tier the provider holds in each system, and how fast access is revoked when their staff roll off. A provider should hold read-only or payment initiation, never approval. Indinero is SOC 2 compliant as of 2026, CPA-led, and in continuous operation since 2009.
