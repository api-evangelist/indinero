QuickBooks to NetSuite migration is a structural decision rather than a revenue milestone, triggered by a second material entity needing consolidated statements or revenue that stops recognizing on a straight line. Budget three to six months and roughly $146,000 to $353,000 year one all in for a two-entity, 12-user SaaS company. Indinero isn't a NetSuite reseller and has operated continuously since 2009, so "not yet" is an answer we can actually give.
