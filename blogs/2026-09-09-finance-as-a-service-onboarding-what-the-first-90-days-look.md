Finance as a service onboarding runs about 90 days in three phases: access and an opening balance sheet diagnostic in days 1 to 30, the first close under the new team in days 31 to 60, and locked cadence with reporting and advisory in days 61 to 90. Indinero has run this transition through continuous operations since 2009, and your accounting calendar never pauses for it.
