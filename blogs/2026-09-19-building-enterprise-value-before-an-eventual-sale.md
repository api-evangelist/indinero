Building business value to sell means raising what an outside party would pay for the whole company, not what it clears in a single year. Enterprise value is sustainable earnings times a multiple, and that multiple reflects risk. Indinero CFO Services, in continuous operations since 2009, focuses on the levers that move both: margin quality, customer concentration, owner dependence, and accrual reporting.
