A fractional CFO for family business owners separates owner economics from operating performance, so profitability can be judged on its own terms. Owner pay, distributions, and related-party rent sit in the same columns as operating results until someone pulls them onto their own lines. Readiness is set by operational complexity, not revenue, and indinero coordinates CFO work with the accounting and tax teams who hold those entries.
