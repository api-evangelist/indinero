Multi entity accounting keeps a standalone ledger for each legal entity, then adds a consolidation layer that eliminates intercompany activity so the group reports only third-party results. Under ASC 810, a parent holding a controlling interest must consolidate. Indinero runs CPA-led entity-level close and consolidation in one engagement, continuous operations since 2009, so your books stay audit-ready from the first entity.
