Foreign subsidiary accounting takes two conversions before consolidation, local GAAP recast onto US GAAP first, then currency translation under ASC 830. The functional currency conclusion at ASC 830-10-45-2 sets every rate that follows, and the cumulative translation adjustment lands in other comprehensive income, not net income.
