Multi-entity consolidation combines a parent and the entities it controls into one set of financial statements under ASC 810. It runs in a fixed order: entity-level close, intercompany reconciliation, eliminations, and consolidated reporting. Indinero's CPA-led team runs that entire sequence as one monthly workflow, so the consolidated numbers hold up to a first audit.
