Controller vs comptroller is a question about sector, not seniority: a controller runs accounting inside a private company, and a comptroller does the identical job in government, public agencies, and nonprofits. A CFO is genuinely different, owning forecasting, capital, and board reporting instead of the close. Indinero bundles bookkeeping, accounting, tax, and fractional CFO advisory under one monthly engagement, so the finance function grows one role at a time.
